## Support Us

Your support helps us continue offering affordable arts education to our community. As a nonprofit organization, we rely on donations, grants, and community partnerships to keep our programs accessible to all.

There are many ways to support En Las Tablas:

- Make a tax-deductible donation
- Volunteer your time and skills
- Attend our performances and events
- Spread the word about our programs
- Partner with us as a community organization

Thank you for supporting our mission to provide high-quality arts education to all!
